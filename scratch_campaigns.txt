FIVE PILLARS OF CHANGE
Five campaigns. One integrated purpose.
ISLAH's five flagship campaigns translate our broader development vision into focused areas of action. Each campaign responds to a distinct dimension of human and community well-being while remaining connected to our integrated approach to development.
From keeping children connected to education and supporting critical healthcare needs, to empowering girls, advancing dignity and access to justice, and preparing communities for the future, our campaigns seek to transform immediate needs into pathways towards lasting opportunity and resilience.
Each campaign represents a long-term commitment to a particular dimension of human and community development, while remaining interconnected with the wider ISLAH ecosystem.
Five campaigns. Multiple pathways. One shared purpose — empowering people and strengthening communities.

01. SHIKSHA NA RUKE
Keeping Education Within Reach
Shiksha Na Ruke is ISLAH's education and learning campaign dedicated to ensuring that children, young people and underserved learners are not forced to discontinue their educational journey because of poverty, social circumstances, lack of resources or limited access to opportunities.
We believe education is more than access to a classroom. It is a pathway to knowledge, confidence, capability, dignity and opportunity.
Through Shiksha Na Ruke, ISLAH seeks to reduce barriers that prevent children and young people from learning, progressing and preparing for their future.
